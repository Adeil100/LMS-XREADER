Event Planning Roles

Roles in the marketing department 

Head of PR 

The head of PR is responsible for public relations (PR). This means they help to tell everyone about the event. They have excellent communication skills and are friendly. 

Head of advertising 

This person is responsible for making the different types of advertising. They are normally very creative and artistic. 

Head of sales 

This person is responsible for selling the tickets for the event. They have good maths and communication skills. 

Head of market research 

This person is responsible for thinking about what will make your event different. They are good at online research and can create reports.

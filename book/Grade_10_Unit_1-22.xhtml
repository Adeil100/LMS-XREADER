Events

Types of events 

There are many different types of events. Some popular ones are: 

Exhibits 

The main purpose of an exhibit is to show something to people.  

Exhibits are usually public. Sometimes you need to buy a ticket to enter.  

Concerts

Concerts are a good way of showing skills like singing, dancing or acting.  

Often people plan a concert to celebrate something. For example, on National Day, students often hold a concert with traditional songs, dances and poetry.  

Festivals

Festivals are events where lots of things are happening. 

For example, there could be shows, exhibits and meetings all happening at one festival. Festivals usually last for a day or longer. Some festivals can last for a month like the Dubai Shopping Festival.  

Competitions

Competitions are a good way of getting people interested in something.  

Lots of businesses and organisations have competitions because it gets people excited about their product. Even if they don’t win, they might decide to buy the product anyway.  

Conferences

A conference is a large meeting where people can talk about a particular subject.  

A conference usually lasts for at least one day or longer.

In this unit, you will learn about events and discuss events you have been to. This unit will focus on travel and tourism. Travel and tourism is important in every country. People from all over the world come to visit the UAE. You will think about all the beautiful places in the UAE. You will also think about the different events held here every year. 

Learning Summary 

By the end of this unit, you will be able to:

identify the purpose of events. 

list different events you have been to. 

understand the importance of travel and tourism.

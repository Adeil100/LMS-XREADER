In this unit, you will learn to work together as a team to plan an event. It is important to be organised and to work together to make sure your event is successful. You will also learn how to evaluate the success of your event. For example, you will evaluate what went well and what you would improve. 

Learning Summary 

By the end of this unit, you will be able to: 

identify the different roles for your event. 

identify the skills that support the roles. 

develop a plan and organise the event. 

hold the event. 

evaluate the success of the event.

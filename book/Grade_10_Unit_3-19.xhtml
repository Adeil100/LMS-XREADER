Team meetings 

A meeting is when your event planning team gets together and talks about the event. Everyone should have a chance to speak at a meeting. You should tell your teammates how you are doing with your tasks. If you have anything that you need help with, you should tell them during the meeting. The manager should listen carefully to everything, and make sure that any problems are solved, so that the event will be a success. It is a good idea to take notes at a meeting, to make sure you remember everything important.

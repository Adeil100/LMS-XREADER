Event Planning Roles

2.2 Management 

Responsibilities of a manager 

A manager has many different responsibilities. Lots of their responsibilities are about how they communicate with other people. A manager should help their team to know their strengths and improve their weaknesses. 

Motivate 

The manager must motivate the members of the team to try their best. They help bring energy to the team to encourage them to get more work done. 

Co-ordinate 

The manager must make sure that everybody knows what their responsibilities are. 

Command 

The manager must set deadlines. This means that people know when they need to finish their work by. 

Communicate 

The manager must be able to speak to people properly. They also need to listen to what their team needs. 

Organise 

The manager must make sure the team have all of the resources they need to make the event a success. Resources are all the things people need to do something. 

Control 

The members of the team must respect the manager. The manager needs to be a good leader. This will mean that the team will listen to what they say.

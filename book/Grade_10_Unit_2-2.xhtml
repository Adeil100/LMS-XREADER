In Term 1, you learned all about your individual skills. In this unit, you will learn about the different roles in event planning. You will also learn about the different skills needed for each role. Events can be difficult to organise, so it is important to have a good team. Each person in the team will focus on a role. Management, finance and marketing are important roles when organising an event. Each role has different responsibilities. 

Learning Summary

By the end of this unit, you will be able to: 

identify the different roles needed for planning events. 

list the different skills needed for each role. 

create marketing materials and calculate budgets. 

understand the role of a manager.

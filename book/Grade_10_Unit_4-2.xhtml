In the final unit of your Business course for Grade 10, you will show what you have learned about yourself during your event. You will think about your role, your skills and what role you would choose for future events. 

Learning Summary 

By the end of this unit, you will be able to:

reflect on your role in this event. 

explain the responsibilities of the different roles. 

list the parts of the event you enjoyed. 

choose the role you prefer. 

identify the skills you have to support your role.

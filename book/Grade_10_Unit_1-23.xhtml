1.2 Travel and tourism events 

The importance of the tourism industry 

There are many interesting things to do, and beautiful places to see, in the UAE. Because of this, many people visit from other countries. They come for holidays with their families, or they come for work. They spend money at businesses like hotels, malls, restaurants and theme parks in the UAE. These businesses are part of the tourism industry. Tourism is important because it creates a lot of jobs, brings money to the country and helps lots of people to know more about the UAE. 

Not all tourists come from abroad. People who live in Dubai like to visit Ras Al Khaimah to go hiking or camping. People in Ajman can go to Abu Dhabi to see the Louvre or the beautiful Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque.

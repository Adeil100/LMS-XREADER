Event Planning Roles

2.4 Finance 

Finance is the money that is needed to support a business or an event. The Head of Finance must record the money coming in and going out. The money going out is called ‘costs’. The money coming in is called ‘revenue’. 

Costs 

It will cost money to have an event. The things you need for your event are called resources. For example, for every event you will need some paper for planning or promotion. This costs money. 

Revenue 

The money that comes into a business is called revenue. For example, the money that you get from selling tickets is called revenue. If you are having a cake sale, the money you get from selling the cakes is called revenue. 

Profit, loss and break even 

Profit is the amount of money you have when you subtract costs from revenue. If the costs are higher than the revenue, then the event will make a loss. If the revenue is equal to the costs, this is called breaking even. If you break even, this means there is no profit and there are no losses. 

Revenue – Costs = Profit/Loss 

If you have profit left after your event, this can be used for something. You can decide this with your teacher and the principal in your school.

Event Planning Roles

2.1 Roles in event planning 

A good team can help to make an event successful. A role is a way to describe the responsibilities each person has in a team. In this section, you will learn about the particular roles that are useful when organising an event. To make sure your event is a success, it really helps if people work together. 

The roles that this unit will focus on are: 

Management

Management is about organising and planning the event. The manager helps connect the team members and make sure everybody knows what their responsibilities are. 

Marketing 

Marketing is about telling people about the event. It is the different ways you can advertise so that people are interested in your event. 

Finance 

Finance is about the money for the event. The financial manager has to think about the costs and the revenue.

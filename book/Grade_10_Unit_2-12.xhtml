Soft skills 

Some soft skills are natural skills. This means you have these skills because they are part of your personality. However, it is important to know that you can improve your soft skills. When you want to improve your skills, you have to practise. It is easier to practise these skills in a team. If someone on your team has better communication skills, you can learn from them. If you would like to learn how to calculate a budget, your classmate could help you to do this.

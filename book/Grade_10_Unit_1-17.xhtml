1.1 Introduction to events 

What is an event?  

An event is something that happens at a planned place or time. People decide to hold an event for many different reasons. Events can be for entertainment, information or to celebrate something. Events can be private which means only people who are invited can go. Private events are things like weddings or celebrations. There are also public events which are for everybody. The UAE has many famous public events every year. Some examples of public events are:  

concerts 

plays 

festivals 

sporting events 

exhibitions

Final preparations 

One or two days before the event, nearly everything should be ready. The manager should check with all team members that everything is prepared. You should check that all the resources you need are available or will be delivered on the day. This is also a good time to do a final marketing campaign, to encourage as many people as possible to come to your event. After one last check that everything is prepared, you are ready to deliver a successful event!  

3.4 Evaluating the event 

After the event has finished, you should think about what went well and what could be improved. This will help you to make future events event better! Think about your event and use smiley faces to show how successful you think the event was.

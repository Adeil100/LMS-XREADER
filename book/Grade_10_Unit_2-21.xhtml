Event Planning Roles

2.3 Marketing 

Marketing is the way of telling people about an event. Marketing gives people information about the event. This is also known as advertising. If you have a good marketing campaign, people will want to come to your event. A campaign is all the types of advertising you use to market your event. 

Effective marketing 

Effective marketing is marketing that works the way you want it to.  

If your marketing is effective, people will: 

know what the event is about. 

know when the event is happening. 

know what the purpose of the event is. 

want to come to the event. 

The AIDA model 

The AIDA model is a way of making sure your marketing is effective. Each letter stands for a word. 

Attention 

This means that people know you are having an event. Your advertising is special, so people notice it. 

Interest 

This means you tell people what makes your event special. 

Desire 

This means you ‘create an emotional connection’. This makes people want to be part of your event.
